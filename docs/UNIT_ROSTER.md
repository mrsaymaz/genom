# Unit roster

Generated by `tools/gen-units.mjs` from `src/data/units.js`. Names and descriptions are player-facing; stats are example values for tuning.

## How units work

- Every nation has four unit lines: **Worker**, **Infantry**, **Ranged** and one **special** line that fits its element. Each line transforms with every age: a new name, a major new look and stronger stats, like the buildings.
- From the Mythic Age, each nation also has one **hero** (Sanctum) and its **signature creature** (Beast Pen). From the Arcane Age, a **siege engine** (Workshop).
- Counters, in plain words: infantry beats fast units; ranged units beat infantry and guardians; fast units (scouts and chargers) beat ranged units; guardians beat infantry and fast units; healers keep others alive. The element cycle (Fire > Earth > Air > Water > Fire) adds a small extra edge.
