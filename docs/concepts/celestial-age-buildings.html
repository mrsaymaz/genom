Celestial Age Buildings

The Celestial Age is the final rebuild: every building takes on its nation's heavens. Hufflepuff builds on clouds under amber stars, Ravenclaw under silver moons and crescents, Gryffindor under suns and phoenix fire, and Slytherin grows giant glowing star-blossoms. Switch any card between all four ages to see the whole climb, and use Silhouette to check each one reads by shape alone.

Celestial style

  • All four ages share one camera and scale on this page, pulled back to fit the Celestial heights. Footprints never change with the age.
  • Each rebuild adds 20% HP over the previous age. Stats are example values for tuning, adjusted by each nation's twist; green is better than the shared baseline and red is worse.
