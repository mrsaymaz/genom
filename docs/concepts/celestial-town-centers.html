Celestial Age Town Centers

The Celestial Age is the final ascent: each Town Center is rebuilt a third time as a seat of the heavens, crowned with a star, a moon, a sun or a living constellation, and gains a third ability. Switch any card between Dark, Mythic, Arcane and Celestial to see the whole climb, and use Silhouette to check each one reads by shape alone.
