Nation Design Languages

Slytherin is the only nation whose buildings look like no one else's. This page explains why, gives the other three a design language as strong as Slytherin's, and tests each one on the Mythic Town Center.

Why Slytherin works

  1. One construction principle. Its buildings are grown, not built. Every choice follows from that.
  2. Its own shapes. Canopies, trunks, roots and standing stones. No other nation uses them.
  3. Its own materials. Bark, moss and old grey stone, never cut blocks or tiles.
  4. Its own light. Green rune glow, mushrooms and fireflies, part of the structure rather than added on.

Why the other three don't

Hufflepuff, Ravenclaw and Gryffindor all use the same kit: stone blocks shaped into boxes and cylinders under cone or gable roofs. Only the colour (pale, blue or red) and the decorations change. Floating islands, glass domes and fire are added on top rather than shaping the building.

The test: hide the colours. In silhouette the current versions read as generic towers, domes and castles that any nation could own, and in the Dark Age they are nearly interchangeable. Switch the prototypes below between Current and New with Silhouette on to compare.

The four languages

One sheet per nation. Each starts from a construction principle, then fixes shapes, materials, light and motifs, and ends with what that nation must never use.

Who owns which motif

Each signature motif belongs to one nation only. If a building needs a motif another nation owns, it gets redesigned until it doesn't.

NationOwnsShared by everyone
HufflepuffWoven domes, hexagons, sails, masts, kites, stripes, amber lightBanners in nation colours, the footprint grid, worker props such as crates, barrels and sacks
RavenclawSpirals, shells, glass, water arcs, bronze instruments, blue light
GryffindorStepped angular stone, spikes, chimneys, open fire and braziers, orange light
SlytherinLiving trees, roots, mounds, standing stones, mushrooms, green light
  • If you approve these languages, the earlier Dark, Mythic and Arcane buildings for Hufflepuff, Ravenclaw and Gryffindor would be rebuilt to match. Slytherin's need only small fixes; for example, its Dark Age burrows already fit.
  • Some current buildings break the ownership table: braziers appear outside Gryffindor and glass domes and floating crystals mix across nations. The rebuild would remove these.
